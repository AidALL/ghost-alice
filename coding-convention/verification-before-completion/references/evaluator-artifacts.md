# Evaluator Artifact Contract

Before claiming a result that adversarial-verification reviewed at level-3-high-stakes, or claiming external governance absorption or evaluator-candidate promotion, the response model locates `evaluator-artifact-contract.md` through `installed-policies.md` in the `_shared` directory beside the host's installed skills, then reads that policy. The policy defines the evaluation procedure and required evidence.

Use the verifier's judgment in `verifier-result.json` under that policy's `verifier_accepted` and `verifier_accepted_candidates` fields for the claimed evaluation. Follow the policy's reading rules for older `accepted` and `accepted_candidates` fields. Missing required evidence leaves the corresponding claim unresolved.
